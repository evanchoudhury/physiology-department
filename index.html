Understanding the body, system by system.

The Department of Physiology at North Bengal Medical College and Hospital introduces medical students to the mechanisms that keep the human body in balance — from cellular function to whole-system regulation.

The department builds a strong foundation in physiology while encouraging scientific thinking, clinical correlation and research-oriented learning. This site presents the department's faculty, academic activities, research interests, facilities and information.

Undergraduate education

Seminars, demonstrations, discussions and departmental sessions that keep teaching active, current and connected to clinical practice.
